# Product

## HELIXNID Carrier Precision Meter V1

### Input
- shipment ID
- ship/purchase time
- carrier handoff time
- original promised ETA
- optional carrier
- optional service
- optional destination / ZIP

### Output
- original ETA
- HELIXNID corrected ETA
- correction days
- late probability
- risk band
- confidence
- matched historical support
- warning window
- model/data evidence IDs

The user-facing product is the measurement layer. The internal HELIXNID evidence architecture remains behind the interface.
