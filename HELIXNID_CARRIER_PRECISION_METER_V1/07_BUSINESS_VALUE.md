# Business Value Measurements

Product 3 converts measured performance into operational counts for:
- 1,000 shipments
- 10,000 shipments
- 100,000 shipments

Measured outputs:
- ETA error-days removed
- extra shipments within ±1 day
- extra shipments within ±2 days
- high-risk shipments flagged
- late shipments captured
- warning lead hours

No dollar savings are invented. Dollar impact is calculated only when a buyer supplies real cost inputs.
